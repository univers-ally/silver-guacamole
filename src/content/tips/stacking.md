---
title: Stacking the same cash promo more than once
order: 30
---

Some promos have an explicit cooloff after you finish them. During it you either get offered credit instead of cash, or the promo doesn't show at all. Two ways to work around that:

1. On promos that only pay out once every item has shipped (`wincash`, for example), put a preorder item in the qualifying order. The promo can't "complete" until that ships, so the cooloff clock doesn't start.
2. Get lucky and get offered the same promo a second time even though you already hit the requirements. This mostly happens with the daily-claim promos, and the two runs may or may not stack side by side. Nobody has reported three at once, so the limit is probably two.
