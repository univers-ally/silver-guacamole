<ul>
  <li>This is when the seller you bought something from adjusts the price. This happens a lot, due to the volatility of the market. It's free money for you!</li>
  <li class="warn">Check all of your orders that have at least reached the Shipped stage (if it's been less than 30 days) every day for free money that is price adjustments. They don't actually have to be delivered for a price adjustment.</li>
  <li>Simply go to the item you ordered, select "Price match/adjustment", and check if the button is orange. If it is, congrats, you can get free money! Click it and get your refund.</li>
  <li class="warn">Note: Temu may or may not let you refund the difference as cash. Apple users are almost always offered a cash option, while Android users are almost never offered one. It depends on Temu's mood or something.</li>
  <li>If you can't find the option to price match, try the three dots menu.</li>
</ul>
