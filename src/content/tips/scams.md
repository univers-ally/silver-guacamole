---
title: How to not get scammed
order: 10
---

The sellers on Temu are scalpers selling at market price. Buying from them supports scalping. If you're going to do it anyway:

- Stick to sellers with over 10k sales and a lot of reviews. That cuts the odds of fake cards and probably nudges the algorithm to show you those sellers more often.
- You usually can't get to a seller's store from the promo page, but you can open the item, tap Chat, and then tap the seller's avatar inside the chat. That drops you on their store page, where you can read more reviews or browse their stock to steer your algorithm a bit.
