Tips and tricks
How to not get scammed

The sellers on Temu are scalpers selling at market price. Buying from them supports scalping. If you're going to do it anyway:

  • Stick to sellers with over 10k sales and a lot of reviews. That cuts the odds of fake cards and probably nudges the algorithm to show you those sellers more often.
  • You usually can't get to a seller's store from the promo page, but you can open the item, tap Chat, and then tap the seller's avatar inside the chat. That drops you on their store page, where you can read more reviews or browse their stock to steer your algorithm a bit.
Not every promo will show up for you

Which ones you get depends on the rules, your region, and your account. If you recently finished a promo, or got a cash payout, your account can be in a cooloff period and the same code will just not work for you. That can last anywhere from a day to over a month. Nothing to do about it except check back later. The codes still work for other accounts.

Getting a promo to show up anyway
  1. Uninstall the app, wait at least 24 hours without logging in or touching the account from anywhere, then reinstall.
  2. Make a new account from a different IP (VPN) and a different device (different MAC address).
  3. Make a new account in a different region (VPN), use a different shipping address inside that region (you can change it at checkout), and do not link a PayPal account that has ever been used on Temu before.
  4. Some promos just won't show up no matter what, and there's no forcing them. Don't lose hope. You'll get out of Temu jail eventually.
Stacking the same cash promo more than once

Some promos have an explicit cooloff after you finish them. During it you either get offered credit instead of cash, or the promo doesn't show at all. Two ways to work around that:

  1. On promos that only pay out once every item has shipped (wincash, for example), put a preorder item in the qualifying order. The promo can't "complete" until that ships, so the cooloff clock doesn't start.
  2. Get lucky and get offered the same promo a second time even though you already hit the requirements. This mostly happens with the daily-claim promos, and the two runs may or may not stack side by side. Nobody has reported three at once, so the limit is probably two.
Timeskipping (speeding up daily claims)

Daily-claim promos go by your phone's clock. We can exploit this by changing the phone's time zone to a place where it's already tomorrow and thus Temu thinks a day has passed. You can burn through every day of most daily-claims/calendars in one day, so never ever pay extra to unlock early unless you can't timeskip.

For it to work correctly, whatever time zone you switch to needs to have since passed midnight since your last claim, otherwise Temu will still think that it's the same day.

The calculator below can be used to tell you which zone to switch to, and when, once you claim something.

  1. Tap the button after claiming
  2. Set your phone's time zone to the city it shows, open Temu, claim again.
  3. Repeat. It's one new claim/zone per hour.

Works on GET100 and may work on other offers. Be careful if you have other daily-claim promos going at the same time, since jumping the date can screw those up too.
